Privacy Policy

DAOS Watch Faces

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Applications Covered

This privacy policy applies to Wear OS watch faces developed and published by DAOS, including Classic Day Date Watch Face.

Information Collection

Our watch faces do not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

They use device-provided information, such as the current time and date, solely to display the watch face.

Data Sharing

We do not share or sell personal information to third parties.

Advertising and Analytics

Our watch faces do not contain advertisements or use analytics services.

Data Retention and Deletion

We do not retain personal information and therefore have no personal information to delete.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy when new watch faces or features are introduced. Changes will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact information provided on our Google Play store listings.
